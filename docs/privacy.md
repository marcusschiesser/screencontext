# ScreenContext Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026. This policy applies to the ScreenContext launch release.

ScreenContext is provided by Marcus Schiesser. For privacy questions, contact [mail@marcusschiesser.de](mailto:mail@marcusschiesser.de).

## Your recordings and context

ScreenContext records the screen or window you select. If you enable the corresponding inputs, it also records system audio, microphone audio, and camera video. The app saves recordings, keyframe images, transcripts, recording history, and templates locally on your Mac.

ScreenContext does not upload your recordings, transcripts, or templates to a ScreenContext server. This App Store configuration does not send analytics events to the developer or an analytics provider. No ScreenContext account is required.

## Transcription

On supported versions of macOS, microphone transcription is processed on your Mac using Apple's speech technology. macOS may need to download speech assets for your chosen language. Apple controls those downloads and its system services.

## Permissions

Screen recording access allows the app to capture your selected screen or window and enabled system audio. Microphone and camera access are used when you enable those inputs. You can manage permissions in macOS System Settings. Recording begins when you start it and ends when you stop it; the floating HUD displays the recording state.

## Sharing with other tools

Copying context or video places material on the macOS clipboard. Other apps you use may read clipboard contents. When you choose an AI handoff, ScreenContext opens the selected external app with prepared context. Your chosen apps and services have their own privacy policies and may process material you share with them. Review the context and the destination before sharing.

## Retention and deletion

Recordings remain in the app's local storage until you delete them. Use Delete Recording in the recording library to remove a saved recording and its associated local artifacts. Templates and preferences remain locally until you change or remove them. Deletion in ScreenContext does not remove copies you have exported, pasted, shared, or backed up elsewhere.

## Support

If you email us, we receive the message, your email address, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive recordings or transcripts. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of your correspondence.

## Changes

We will update this policy if the app's privacy practices change. The published policy will identify its effective date.
