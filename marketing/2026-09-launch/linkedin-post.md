I built ScreenContext to simplify sharing screencasts with my AI agents.

I use these recordings to ask agents to polish videos, create issues, or make changes.

ScreenContext — Screen context for AI agents.

Start a recording from your agent, finish it and then click Copy & Return to use the recording in your agent.

In this video, I ask for a shorter website heading and a button that’s easier to see.

The launch video itself was created with ScreenContext recordings, too.

It’s free for Mac, open source, and now on the App Store.
