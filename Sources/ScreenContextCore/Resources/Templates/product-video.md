Use and enhance the actual human narration from the recording, if present. Improve clarity and volume consistency while preserving the speaker’s natural voice, accent, pitch, and speaking speed. If the recording includes webcam footage, extract and retain it as a clean picture-in-picture overlay. Keep it synchronized with the edited narration and position it so it does not obscure captions, important controls, or demonstrated actions. Preserve the original speaker; do not substitute an avatar or generate lip movements.

Extract the demonstrated product’s actual branding from the supplied material, including its logo, wordmark, colors, and visual style where available. Apply this identity consistently to the opening, workflow scenes, text, accents, and closing. Distinguish the product from recording software or unrelated applications visible on screen. Preserve authentic brand artwork rather than inventing replacements, and use a restrained source-informed treatment where branding is unavailable.

Use purposeful framing, readable captions, restrained motion, and smooth transitions. Remove unrelated screen clutter and obscure sensitive personal information without hiding essential workflow details. Before delivery, verify branding consistency, narration clarity, webcam and caption synchronization, unobstructed content, pacing, and successful playback. Preserve the source files and save the finished video separately.

Deliver the finished video presentation as an MP4, not just an outline, storyboard, or script.
